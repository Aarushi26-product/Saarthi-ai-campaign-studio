How do you want to say it?
The hook structure is the single most important decision. It determines whether your post gets saved and shared — or ignored. Each maps to a proven virality pattern.
💥
Myth Bust
Counter a wrong belief most people have. Flips thinking instantly. Drives the highest comment count.
😲
Surprising Fact / Stat
A specific counter-intuitive data point. Makes people say "wait, really?" Stops the scroll cold.
⚠️
Common Mistake
"Yeh galti main khud karta tha." Personal, relatable. Highest share-to-friend rate.
🤫
What Nobody Tells You
The insider angle. Makes followers feel they're getting access to a secret the market hides.
📅
Why This Matters TODAY
Connect the concept to what's happening in markets right now. High urgency, high saves.
📋
Step-by-Step Guide
Numbered, actionable. "4 cheezein jo aaj hi karo." Instagram's highest-save format.

My Honest Opinion
Your direct POV without hedging. More polarising = more shareable. Builds personal brand trust.
💡
Simple Explanation
Explain one complex concept simply using an everyday Indian analogy. Rachana's signature format.
⚖️
Comparison / VS
"X vs Y — which one should you pick?" Extremely high saves. People bookmark for reference.
📖
Client Story
A real situation you witnessed (anonymised). Shows impact, builds trust, humanises your expertise.

🎥 Video Prompt — Copy to HeyGen
📋 How to use in HeyGen:
1. Create your Digital Twin (one-time setup): In HeyGen → "Avatars" → "Create Digital Twin" → Record a 15-second webcam clip (face clearly visible, good lighting). This captures your face, motion style, and consent. HeyGen uses this for both talking-head AND full-body cinematic shots.
2. Clone your voice (one-time setup): "Voices" → "Voice Clone" → Record 2-3 minutes of yourself speaking naturally in your language (Hinglish/Hindi/English). This ensures the video sounds like YOU.
3. Open HeyGen Dashboard → Click "Create Video" → Select "Video Agent (Prompt to Video)"
4. Paste the entire video prompt above into the prompt text box
5. Video Agent automatically uses:
   • Seedance 2.0 for walking/cinematic scenes (your avatar walks, changes locations, full body motion)
   • Avatar V for talking-head explanation scenes (close-up, lip-sync, hand gestures)
   • It mixes both engines automatically based on the scene descriptions in the prompt
6. Set aspect ratio to 9:16 for Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts, or 16:9 for LinkedIn/YouTube
7. Click "Generate" — HeyGen will produce the video in 3-5 minutes
8. Review in AI Studio: Every scene is editable after rendering. Swap a background, adjust a motion, or tweak the script — without regenerating the full video.
9. Download and post on your social media with the caption from the Instagram/LinkedIn tab above
